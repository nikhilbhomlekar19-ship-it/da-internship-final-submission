# Enterprise BI Suite - one-page user guide

**What it is.** One report for sales, finance, supply chain and HR. You see only the regions you manage; executives see all.

**Open it.** Power BI app *Enterprise BI* (link from your manager/BI team). Data refreshes daily at 03:00 IST; the card *Data Through* shows the last sales date (AdventureWorks sample data ends 29 Jun 2025).

| Page | Use it to... |
|---|---|
| Executive Overview | Seven headline KPIs, monthly trend with rolling 3-month average and prior year, territory scorecard |
| Sales Performance | Net sales by channel, category/subcategory (click **+** to expand), top products |
| Finance | Net sales → COGS → gross margin %, billed amounts, margin vs payroll |
| Supply Chain | Purchase spend, on-time delivery, fill and reject rate, vendors, inventory below reorder point |
| HR | Month-end headcount, new hires, tenure, estimated payroll by department |

**Filter.** *Fiscal Year* and *Territory* slicers stay in sync across pages. Click a bar or table row to cross-filter the page; click again to clear.

**Drill down.** Right-click a product, customer or vendor in a table → **Drill through → Product / Customer / Vendor Detail**. Use the arrow at the top-left of a detail page to go back.

**Definitions.** *Fiscal year* runs July-June (FY2025 = Jul 2024-Jun 2025). *Net sales* = line sales after discounts. *Gross margin %* = (net sales − COGS) ÷ net sales, COGS at the standard cost valid on the order date. *YoY %* compares the same period one year earlier - use fiscal-year or month views (a partial calendar year is not comparable). *Rolling 3M* = average monthly net sales of the last three months. *On-time %* = shipped on or before due date. *Headcount* = employees at the last month-end of the selected period. *Payroll* is an **estimate** (hourly rate × 173.33 h).

**Good to know.** Inventory is company-wide (not split by region). Exports and "Analyze in Excel" respect your region access. If a number looks wrong, note the page, filters and time, and contact the BI team (support contact: *add e-mail here*).
